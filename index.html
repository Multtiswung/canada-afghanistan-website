It Wasn't Simply "Canada vs Afghanistan"

Canada's involvement began after the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks. Canada joined an international coalition whose initial military campaign targeted al-Qaeda and the Taliban regime in Afghanistan.

🇨🇦

Canada

Contributed naval forces, aircraft, special forces and later conventional troops to the international mission.

×
🇦🇫

Afghanistan

A country already affected by decades of conflict. In 2001, the Taliban controlled much of the country.

⚡ Quick Facts

A few important facts to understand the events of 2001.

📅

September 11, 2001

Al-Qaeda carried out terrorist attacks in the United States, killing nearly 3,000 people.

🇨🇦

October 7, 2001

Canada announced that it would contribute air, land and sea forces to the international campaign.

⚓

Operation APOLLO

Canada's contribution included naval forces operating in the Arabian Sea and surrounding region.

🪖

Special Forces

Canadian special forces members arrived in Afghanistan in late 2001.

🌍

International Mission

Canada's role was part of a much larger international effort, rather than a standalone war between two countries.

⏳

Long-Term Mission

Canada's involvement eventually lasted more than a decade, ending its military mission in 2014.

🕰️ The Timeline

Follow the key events that connected Canada to the conflict.

September 11, 2001

9/11 Attacks

Al-Qaeda terrorists attack the United States. The attacks become the catalyst for a major international response.

October 7, 2001

Canada Joins the Campaign

Canada announces that it will contribute military forces to the international campaign against terrorism.

Late 2001

Canadian Special Forces Arrive

Canadian special forces members arrive in Afghanistan to support multinational counter-terrorism operations.

February 2002

Canadian Battle Group Arrives

The first major Canadian army contingent arrives in Afghanistan and operates alongside American forces.

2003–2014

Mission Expands

Canada's role evolves through security, combat, reconstruction and training missions before the military mission ends in 2014.
